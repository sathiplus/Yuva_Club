Privacy and Trust

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This policy explains the categories of information Yuva Club uses to operate supervised youth leadership programs.

Information we collect

Registration and account information may include a student name, date of birth, contact information, parent or guardian information, program participation, submissions, attendance, certificates, and approved feedback.

How information is used

Information is used to administer accounts, provide program activities, support student safety, communicate with families, track authorized progress, and operate and secure the service.

Student safety

School-age participation requires parent or guardian involvement. Access is limited by role, and official AI-assisted feedback remains subject to authorized human review.

Sharing

Personal information is not sold. Information may be handled by service providers needed to operate the platform, or disclosed when required for safety, security, or legal compliance.

Retention and security

Information is retained only as needed for program, safety, operational, and legal purposes. Administrative, technical, and access controls are used to protect it.

Your choices

A parent, guardian, or eligible account holder may request access, correction, or deletion where applicable by using the contact method on the Contact page.

Questions

For privacy questions or requests, use the verified contact route on our Contact page. Do not include passwords, access codes, or sensitive student records in an initial message.
