Verified public contact route

Contact information should be trustworthy, not improvised.

Until a dedicated YUVA support mailbox is formally verified, visit the official YUVA Club website for current organization contact information.

Never include a password, date of birth, access code, private student submission, or other sensitive record in an initial message.

Urgent safety concerns

Immediate danger requires immediate local help.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service. Platform safety concerns should also be reported through the authorized YUVA Club account workflow.
