Contact Yuva Club

Yuva Club is a KarmaBro youth leadership platform. Until a dedicated support mailbox is formally verified, use the official KarmaBro website below.

Visit www.karmabro.com for current organization contact information.

Account safety

Never send a password, date of birth, access code, private student submission, or other sensitive record in an initial support message.

Urgent safety concerns

If someone is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service. Platform safety concerns should also be reported through the authorized Yuva Club account workflow.
