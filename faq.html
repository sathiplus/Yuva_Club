The experience

What is YUVA Club?

YUVA Club is a youth leadership platform and community that helps young people lead with confidence, communicate with purpose, think critically, strengthen character, and create positive change through presentations, challenges, guided practice, and reflection.

Is YUVA only for confident speakers?

No. YUVA is designed for growth, not for students who already feel confident. Students can begin where they are and develop through supported practice.

Can shy students participate?

Yes. A quiet beginning does not limit leadership potential. Students are encouraged to take meaningful steps at an appropriate pace and grow through experience.

How does AI Mentor work?

For eligible premium accounts, AI Mentor presents approved feedback connected to existing presentation reviews. It supports reflection and improvement; it is not a conversational companion and does not replace human mentors or educators.

Are students judged by AI?

No. AI-assisted feedback can support learning, but leadership levels, approvals, and protected decisions remain subject to the platform’s existing human-controlled workflows.

Access and growth

What is included free?

Free registration includes the first two presentations, Practice Studio, Presentation Studio, Leadership Journey, and Challenges. AI Mentor is not included in the free experience.

What happens after the first two presentations?

Students may continue their journey through Premium, which can include AI Mentor, unlimited presentations, advanced coaching, certificates, a leadership portfolio, parent insights, and additional premium features.

Is AI Mentor included in the free experience?

No. AI Mentor is a premium experience and is not advertised as part of free registration.

How are leadership levels approved?

Practice and feedback support growth, but advancement requires approval by an administrator, mentor, or judge so levels and recognition remain meaningful.

Can parents see progress?

Authorized parents can use the existing Parent Portal to view information for children linked to their account. Access remains subject to authentication and parent-child authorization.

Trust and partnership

Can schools and organizations use YUVA?

YUVA is intended to support schools, libraries, community centers, nonprofits, and youth organizations. Organizations can request a demo through the existing Contact route.

How is student safety and privacy handled?

YUVA uses existing role-based access, approvals, authorization checks, and published privacy and safety information. Sensitive information should never be shared through unapproved channels. Review the Safety information and Privacy Policy for current public guidance.
